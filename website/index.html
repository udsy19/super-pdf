How it works

Three steps. That is the whole app.

Open a PDF

One document, one thumbnail sidebar, in the window you already know from Preview.

Press Tools

Search or pick a tile. “Make smaller”, not “Optimize PDF”. ⌘T opens it from the keyboard.

Press the big button

The page itself is the live preview. Your original is never overwritten without asking, and every change can be undone.

Make smaller

Shrink a file so it’s easy to send.

Pick Good, Email or Best quality. Each one shows the size you will get before you press the button.

  • The pages stay the same, with comments, form entries and bookmarks kept
  • A new copy is saved next to the original

Questions

Good to know

Is Super PDF really free?

Yes. It is free, with no pricing, no trial and no account, and the source is open under the MIT licence. Read it, build it or change it on GitHub.

Does it use AI, or send my files anywhere?

No AI, no account, no server and no telemetry. Your files are opened, changed and saved on your Mac. Text recognition runs on Apple Vision. The only network traffic is the update check, and PRIVACY.md lists all of it. You can turn that off in Settings › Privacy.

Will it overwrite my original file?

Never without asking. Tools save a copy next to the original, such as “Lease agreement (smaller).pdf”, and every change can be undone.

Why does macOS ask me to confirm the first time?

The app is signed with its own certificate rather than notarised by Apple. The first time you open it, macOS asks you to confirm in System Settings › Privacy & Security. The DMG’s release notes show the steps.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. The download is one universal app.

Can it turn a PDF into Word or Excel?

Yes. Conversions run natively where macOS can do them, through LibreOffice if it is installed on your Mac, and through Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages, Numbers or Keynote when you have them. Nothing is uploaded.

How do I update or remove it?

It keeps itself up to date from this repository’s releases, and Super PDF › Check for Updates… always works. To remove it, run curl -fsSL https://udsy19.github.io/super-pdf/install.sh | bash -s -- --uninstall.

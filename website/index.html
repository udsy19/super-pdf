What do you want to do?

All 52 tools, in the same eight groups as the Tools sheet in the app. Each has a plain name and opens one small panel with one button.

Works with Wi-Fi off.

Your files are opened, changed and saved on your Mac, never uploaded. There’s no account, no AI and no analytics. The one thing Super PDF connects to is GitHub, to check for updates, and you can turn that off.

Questions

Anything else? Ask on GitHub.

Is it really free?

Yes. There’s no price, no trial and no account, and every tool is included. The code is open under the MIT licence, so you can read it, build it or change it on GitHub.

Does it upload my files or use AI?

No. Files are opened, changed and saved on your Mac. Super PDF has no AI, no server and no analytics. The only network traffic is the update check, and the privacy statement lists all of it.

Will it change my original file?

Not without asking. Tools save a new copy next to the original, such as “Lease agreement (smaller).pdf”, and every change can be undone.

Can it turn a PDF into Word or Excel?

Yes, and into PowerPoint, pictures, text, EPUB and PDF/A. It converts natively where macOS can, and uses LibreOffice, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages, Numbers or Keynote when they’re on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.

How do I update or remove it?

It keeps itself up to date from GitHub Releases, and Super PDF › Check for Updates… always works. To remove it, run curl -fsSL https://udsy19.github.io/super-pdf/install.sh | bash -s -- --uninstall.
